# Hook lab

In a feed there are 1–3 seconds. People do not refuse ads, they refuse the **boring**. Attention is
an exchange: the viewer gives time when they see at once what they get back.

Read the product card first (see `product-marketing`); take the audience's verbatim phrases from it,
they make the best hooks. Reply in the user's language.

## 1. Ten hooks, then choose

Write ten first lines (or first-frame texts) for the idea:
- 3 questions about a pain the card names;
- 3 bold claims (a contrarian claim from the narrative works well);
- 2 before/after results;
- 2 with a specific number.

Score each 1–5 on: stops the scroll, specific, honest (the video delivers it), fits the platform.
Recommend the best two for an A/B test, and say why.

## 2. The script: Hook → Retain → Reward → Action

| Step | Job | Tools |
|---|---|---|
| Hook (0–3 s) | Stop the thumb | Unexpected claim, before/after, a question about the pain, motion in frame, text on the first frame |
| Retain | Open a curiosity loop | "The third one changed everything", a list, a story with conflict, quick cuts |
| Reward | Close the loop with value | A tip they can use today, the answer, the punchline |
| Action | One simple next step | "Save it", "comment the word X", "link in bio" |

Deliver a table: time, what is on screen, what is said, the on-screen text. Subtitles always (most
watch muted, and it is accessibility). Vertical 9:16.

## 3. Copy formulas for posts and pages

- **AIDA**: Attention → Interest → Desire → Action. Landing pages and ads.
- **PAS**: Problem → Agitate ("and here is why it's worse than it looks") → Solution. Short posts.
- **Before / After / Bridge**: how it is now, how it could be, what leads from one to the other.

## 4. What makes the product interesting

- Show, don't tell: the product working for 5 s beats 30 s of description.
- Specific: "saves 3 hours a week", not "saves time".
- A person on camera: a face and a voice give trust motion graphics lack, more so as AI content
  grows (35% of US consumers rely less on influencers because of it, Gartner 2026).
- Proof in frame: a real screen, a stopwatch, a real result.

## Classic example

Dollar Shave Club (2012): the founder on camera, an absurd humorous line, a clear offer; a tiny
budget and about 12,000 orders in 48 hours. Hook, personality and simple value, not production.

## Guardrails

The video must deliver what the hook promises. No fake urgency, no invented results, mark paid
partnerships.
