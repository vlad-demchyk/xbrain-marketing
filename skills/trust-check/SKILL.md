# Trust check

People do not mind ads; they mind ads that **interrupt, lie or do not concern them**. Good marketing
feels like useful advice from someone you trust. Reply in the user's language.

## 1. Dark patterns (block release until fixed)

- Fake scarcity or urgency: timers that reset, "3 left" that never changes, invented deadlines.
- Hidden costs, pre-ticked add-ons, a price that changes at checkout.
- Hard cancellation: cancelling must be as easy as signing up. Amazon paid $2.5B to settle the FTC's
  Prime case (Sep 2025); the EU Digital Services Act bans dark patterns on platforms.
- Confirmshaming ("No, I don't want to save money").
- Invented testimonials, numbers, logos or screenshots; reviews from people who did not use it.
- Undisclosed paid partnerships. 70% of consumers react negatively (BBB National Programs, 2025).

## 2. Honest use of influence principles

Reciprocity, social proof, authority, liking, commitment, scarcity, unity (Cialdini) are fine when
true: a free template, reviews from the same segment, real limits (seats in a cohort, a real date).
Flag each use of a principle and whether it is true.

## 3. Promise match

The ad's hook, the page's first screen and what the product does in the first minutes say the same
thing. List each mismatch.

## 4. The six expectations of the user

1. **Relevance**: about my problem, when it matters.
2. **Honesty**: labelled ads, real results, no fine print.
3. **Value now**: useful even without buying.
4. **A human face**: a real person and real experience, more so amid AI content.
5. **Native**: looks like the platform it is on.
6. **Respect for choice**: easy to close, unsubscribe, cancel.

## 5. Awareness stage fit (Eugene Schwartz)

| Stage | In their head | Show them |
|---|---|---|
| Unaware | "All is fine" | Stories about the situation, no product |
| Problem aware | "How do I deal with this?" | Teaching content, approaches compared |
| Solution aware | "Which one?" | Cases, reviews, comparison with alternatives, demo |
| Product aware | "What if it doesn't fit?" | Trial, guarantee, answers to objections |
| Customer | "Did I choose right?" | Fast first result, support, community |

Say which stage the piece targets and whether it fits the channel's audience.

## Output

A table: issue, where, severity (block / fix soon / note), the fix. Then what is already done well.
