## Platform signals (what the ranking rewards)

- Instagram (Adam Mosseri, January 2025): watch time, likes per reach, sends per reach. Likes weigh a
  bit more for followers, sends a bit more for non-followers. Claims like "sends weigh 3–5× likes"
  are not traceable to Mosseri.
- LinkedIn: dwell time (documented on LinkedIn's engineering blog), substantive comments, native
  formats. External links in the post body cut reach; estimates range 18–60% (unofficial). Separate
  vertical video feed since late 2024.
- TikTok: about half of US consumers use TikTok as a search engine (Adobe Express, Jan 2026, n=807;
  41% in 2024). Words in the title, on-screen text and speech work like SEO.
- TikTok Shop: sales come mostly from affiliate creators on commission and from live streams; short
  video brings reach and first purchase, live sells bundles and higher prices. Visual, impulse-priced
  products lead (beauty, home, accessories). US 2025 sales $15.8B (eMarketer); other totals conflict.
- Reddit: practitioners put the time to meaningful results at 4–5 months of genuine participation
  (vendor guide). Text ads that read like posts beat display-style ads for B2B/SaaS (vendor guide).

## Platform audiences

- Instagram: 3 billion monthly users (Zuckerberg, 24 Sep 2025).
- Threads: 500 million monthly users (Meta, June 2026); 150M+ daily (Q3 2025 call).
- Reddit: 130.3 million daily users, +18% YoY; ad revenue $762M, +64% YoY (Q2 2026 results).
- Reddit users: 82% trust recommendations from other Reddit users (Reddit's own survey, 2025).
- LinkedIn and TikTok: aggregator figures conflict (LinkedIn 1–1.4B registered members; TikTok
  1.7–2B monthly). Use the companies' own pages before citing.

## Conversion

- Landing pages, all industries: 6.6% median; SaaS 3.8% (lowest); ecommerce 4.2%; education 8.4%;
  events 12.3%. (Unbounce, 41,000 pages; many "2026" roundups repackage it)
- B2B software, visitor → signup: free trial 4.5%, freemium 9%. (ChartMogul, Growth Unhinged,
  ProductLed, Jan 2026, 200 products)
- Trial → paid: card required 48.8%, no card 18.2%, freemium 2.6%. (same survey)
- Demo-request pages from cold traffic: 1.5–4% (agency estimates).

## Ads, creators and content

- US creator-driven advertising ~$44B in 2026 (from $37B in 2025). (IAB)
- 61% of marketers worldwide plan to raise creator/influencer budgets in 2026. (Kantar)
- Micro and nano creators take 45.5% of influencer spend in 2026. (eMarketer forecast)
- Meta Partnership Ads: −19% cost per acquisition, +13% CTR in tests with ~2,400 advertisers. (Meta)
- Creator content is ~44% of paid media creative; 92% of leaders use it in paid social. (CreatorIQ
  survey of 100 leaders, vendor)
- 77% of brands repurpose creator content in paid ads. (Aspire 2026, vendor)
- Short-form video is the top ROI format for 48.6% of marketers; long video 28.6%, live 25.1%, UGC
  24%, blog posts 22.3%. (HubSpot 2026)
- Digital video ad spend $72.4B in 2025, +14%. Short-form video ads ~$111B globally in 2025.
- "UGC converts 10× better", "92% trust UGC": vendor claims that quote each other. Do not cite.

## AI search (GEO)

- 82% of links cited by AI engines are earned media, not brand sites. (Muck Rack, Dec 2025)
- 12% of URLs cited by AI assistants rank in Google's top 10; ~80% are not in the top 100. (Ahrefs,
  Aug 2025)
- Reddit ranks #1 or #2 among cited domains on all five major AI platforms. (Peec AI, Mar 2026,
  vendor)
- Citation shares swing hard: Reddit in ChatGPT answers fell from ~60% to ~10% between early August
  and mid-September 2025. (Semrush) Spread presence across many sources.
- Only 16% of brands track AI search visibility systematically. (Erlin, vendor)

## Trust and attitudes to advertising

- 35% of US consumers rely less on influencers because of AI-generated content. (Gartner, Sep 2026,
  n=1,006)
- 70% feel negatively about influencers who hide paid relationships; trust in company ads 87% vs
  influencer ads 74%. (BBB National Programs, 2025)
- Trust: micro-influencers 3.09/5 vs paid influencers 2.72/5. (TIA 2026 survey)

## Regulation (dark patterns)

- EU Digital Services Act bans dark patterns on online platforms.
- Amazon agreed to pay $2.5B ($1B civil penalty, $1.5B refunds) to settle the FTC case over Prime
  enrollment and cancellation (25 Sep 2025), without admitting wrongdoing.

## Classic cases (widely reported, company-sourced)

- Dropbox referral (2008–2010): 500 MB to both sides, prompts at signup and near the storage limit;
  ~100k → 4M users in 15 months.
- Dollar Shave Club launch video (2012): ~12,000 orders in 48 hours, low budget, founder on camera.
- Liquid Death: water positioned against energy drinks and beer at concerts, not against water.
- Duolingo and Ryanair on TikTok: brands acting as creators, a character with a voice.
- Notion: user-made templates as free product demos; Glossier: customers' photos as the brand look;
  Apple "Shot on iPhone": user photos as proof.
