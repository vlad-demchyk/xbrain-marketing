# Platform pack

Every network is its own culture. The same idea lands in different packaging. Pick the platform
where the audience **already looks** for what the product offers; one or two done well beat five
done thinly.

Read the product card first (see `product-marketing`). Reply in the user's language; write the
posts in the language of the audience the card names.

## 1. Choose platforms

| The product is… | Start with |
|---|---|
| B2B, services, SaaS for companies, hiring | LinkedIn + Reddit |
| A visual consumer product, lifestyle, local business | Instagram + TikTok |
| A mass product for a young audience, entertainment, learning | TikTok + Instagram Reels |
| A technical or niche tool for developers and enthusiasts | Reddit + Threads/X |
| A founder's or expert's personal brand, opinions, writing | Threads + LinkedIn |

Say why, in one line per platform, using the card's audience.

## 2. Load the platform's playbook

Read only the playbooks for the chosen platforms:
[LinkedIn](references/linkedin.md) · [Instagram](references/instagram.md) ·
[Threads](references/threads.md) · [Reddit](references/reddit.md) · [TikTok](references/tiktok.md)

Each gives the audience, what works, the tone, what fails, what the ranking rewards, the success
metric and a real example. Numbers come from `marketing-benchmarks`; cite them with source and year.

## 3. One idea, many formats

Take one idea (a result, a lesson, a contrarian claim from the card's narrative) and pack it per
platform. Example, the idea "we cut onboarding from 7 steps to 2 and signups grew":

| Platform | Packaging |
|---|---|
| LinkedIn | Carousel: "5 steps we removed from onboarding and what happened to the numbers" |
| Instagram | Reel: screen recording before/after with on-screen text |
| Threads | "Removed 5 signup steps. The scariest to cut was X. Which would you cut first?" |
| Reddit | A detailed post in the relevant subreddit with numbers and mistakes, no product link in the body |
| TikTok | "POV: you're the designer and they tell you to cut 5 steps" + humour + the result |

Deliver for each platform: the ready text (or script), the visual direction, the call to action,
and the metric to check after a week.

## 4. The system around the posts

1. **Profile as a landing page**: who you are, whom you help, one call to action (a lead magnet).
2. **3–4 content pillars**: expertise, process and behind the scenes, customer cases, personal.
3. **Rhythm over polish**: three posts a week for a year beat three a day for a month. Skipping a
   week costs growth (Buffer's "no-post penalty").
4. **Conversations**: reply to every comment in the first 30–60 minutes; comment on others in the
   niche. Replies correlate with +42% engagement on Threads, +30% on LinkedIn.
5. **Owned channel**: move people from the network to a newsletter or a community; the algorithm
   can change any day.
6. **Amplify**: run the best organic posts as ads or through creators (see `ads`, `ad-creative`).

## Guardrails

- Mark paid partnerships. Never invent testimonials, numbers or screenshots.
- On Reddit and in communities, say you are the maker. Follow each community's self-promotion rules.
- Do not cross-post the same text everywhere; adapt it.
