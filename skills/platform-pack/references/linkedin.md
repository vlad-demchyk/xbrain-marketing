# LinkedIn

- **Audience**: professionals, managers, founders, recruiters, thinking about careers and business.
- **Works**: document carousels (PDF slides) with step-by-step advice; personal stories with a
  business lesson; data and cases with numbers; a well-argued opinion against the consensus.
  Native vertical video reaches non-followers through the video feed.
- **Tone**: expert but human. The first two lines decide whether people click "see more".
- **Fails**: a link in the post body (link posts 3.81% vs carousels 21.77%, Buffer 2026; reach cuts
  estimated at 18–60%, unofficial). Put the link in the first comment or the profile. Corporate
  press-release language.
- **Ranking rewards**: dwell time (LinkedIn documents it), substantive comments, native formats.
  Carousels hold attention longer, which is why they win.
- **Success metric**: comments from target people, DMs, profile views, connection requests from the
  segment.
- **Example**: B2B founders posting from their personal profile rather than the company page; the
  personal profile usually reaches far more people.

## Carousel template (8–10 slides)

1. Hook: a promise or a surprising number. 2. Why it matters. 3–8. One step or idea per slide, a
short headline plus one line. 9. Summary. 10. Call to action (comment a word, follow, the link in
the comments).
