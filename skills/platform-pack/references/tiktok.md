# TikTok

- **Audience**: mass audience; the recommendation feed shows content far beyond followers.
- **Works**: 15–60 s videos with a hook in the first second; trends and sounds adapted to the niche;
  short tutorials; a brand character with a voice; video replies to comments; the product in real
  use.
- **Tone**: light, fast, authentic. Shot on a phone often beats a studio.
- **Fails**: a recut TV ad; a slow start; horizontal video.
- **TikTok as search**: about half of US consumers search on TikTok (Adobe, Jan 2026). Put the words
  people type ("how to choose…", "best … for …", "… review") in the title, on-screen text and speech.
- **TikTok Shop** (where available): affiliate creators on commission and live streams drive sales.
  Short video brings reach and the first purchase, live sells bundles. Visual, impulse-priced
  products lead.
- **Success metric**: completion and retention, shares, comments, brand searches after the video.
- **Examples**: Duolingo's owl with a personality; Ryanair's self-irony about cheap flights. Brands
  acting as creators, not advertisers.
