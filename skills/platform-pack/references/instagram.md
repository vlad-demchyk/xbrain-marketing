# Instagram

- **Audience**: 3 billion monthly users; strong visual and lifestyle focus.
- **Works**: Reels to reach new people; carousels for saves and engagement of followers; Stories for
  the warm audience and sales (polls, stickers, links); Collab posts with creators.
- **Reels or carousel**: Reels reach 1.36× more people, carousels get about twice the engagement
  rate (6.90% vs 3.31% of reach, Buffer 2026). Reels for discovery, carousels for followers.
- **Tone**: aesthetic or honest behind-the-scenes; recognisable as the brand either way.
- **Fails**: a product shot on white with no story; hashtags as a strategy.
- **Ranking rewards** (Adam Mosseri): watch time, likes per reach, sends per reach. Likes weigh a bit
  more for followers, sends for non-followers. Design posts people want to send to a friend.
- **Success metric**: sends and saves over likes; watch time; profile visits and DMs.
- **Example**: Glossier built a beauty brand on customers' photos and reviews rather than models;
  Apple "Shot on iPhone" turns user photos into billboards.

## Reel structure (15–45 s)

Hook in the first second (text on the first frame), the payoff promised early, one idea, subtitles
always (most watch muted), a loop or a call to send it to someone.
