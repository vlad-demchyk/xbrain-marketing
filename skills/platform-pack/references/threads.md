# Threads

- **Audience**: 500 million monthly users (June 2026); the Instagram audience in a conversation mood.
  Strong topics: tech, design, creative work, personal brands. Topic communities exist.
- **Works**: short thoughts and observations from experience; questions to the audience; threaded
  breakdowns; replies in other people's threads; posting into topic communities.
- **Format**: visuals do better on average (video 5.55%, image 4.55%, text 2.79%, link 2.34%,
  Buffer 2026), but the spread is wide: a sharp text opinion can still take off.
- **Tone**: conversational, informal, like talking to a colleague.
- **Fails**: open advertising; a LinkedIn post pasted without adaptation; links in the main post.
- **Success metric**: replies (real conversations), new followers, reposts and quotes.
- **Habit that pays**: reply to everyone. Posts with creator replies saw +42% engagement (Buffer,
  128k posts, correlation).
- **Example**: makers posting one idea from their work every day and answering every reply.
